How Worthwise works

Four steps from a scattered bank balance to a clear decision.

Every purchase runs through the same loop. You supply the context once; Worthwise does the reasoning every time after.

  1. 01

    Track

    Log a purchase in seconds. No spreadsheets, no screenshots.

  2. 02

    Understand

    See where your money actually goes — and what's really left over.

  3. 03

    Plan

    Set goals and see the real cost of every decision in months, not vibes.

  4. 04

    Improve

    Get a clear verdict — BUY, WAIT, or SKIP — with the maths behind it.
