How Worthwise works

Four steps from a scattered bank balance to a clear decision.

Every purchase runs through the same loop. You supply the context once; Worthwise does the reasoning every time after.

  1. 01

    Track

    Log a purchase in seconds. No spreadsheets, no screenshots.

  2. 02

    Understand

    See where your money actually goes — and what's really left over.

  3. 03

    Plan

    Set goals and see the real cost of every decision in months, not vibes.

  4. 04

    Improve

    Get a clear verdict — BUY, WAIT, or SKIP — with the maths behind it.

What's inside

Everything you need to reason about money — nothing you don't.

Smart spending analysis

Automatic categorisation and plain-English insight into where your money is going — with the patterns you'd miss on your own.

Financial insights

Know your real surplus, not just your balance. Worthwise separates what's yours from what's already committed.

Goal tracking

Set targets — a trip, an emergency fund, a deposit — and watch them move as your income and spending change.

Purchase decisions

Every purchase gets a verdict and the reasoning behind it. No black boxes, no "trust the algorithm."

Personal overview

One place for income, expenses, savings, and commitments — the full picture behind every recommendation.

Progress visualisation

Timelines, dials, and before/after comparisons that make the numbers feel real — not like a spreadsheet with a coat of paint.

Goals, not wishlists

A goal is only useful if you can see it move.

Every purchase you weigh shows its impact on your goals in months. Wait three weeks, put down 20%, split the payment — Worthwise tells you what each choice actually costs you.

  • ◆ Trade-offs you can act on, not just warnings
